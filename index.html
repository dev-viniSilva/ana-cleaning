Good to Know

Questions, Answered

Quick answers about how we work. Anything else, Ana is one text away.

(717) 623-6050
What areas do you serve?

We clean homes in Monmouth County, Ocean County and parts of Middlesex County, NJ: every town within about 25 miles of Long Branch, including Red Bank, Asbury Park, Freehold, Toms River and Lakewood. If your town isn't listed, call or text and we'll let you know.

What types of cleaning do you offer?

Standard house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in and move-out cleaning, after-construction cleaning, and Airbnb and short-term rental turnovers.

What's the difference between a standard and a deep clean?

A standard clean keeps a tidy home fresh: surfaces, floors, kitchen and bathrooms. A deep clean goes further into the details, like baseboards, vents and behind appliances. A first visit always begins with a deep clean, then we maintain it on every return visit.

How do I get a quote or book a cleaning?

Send the request form, call or text (717) 623-6050, or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us your home size and preferred date, and Ana will follow up personally within a few hours to confirm the details.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. Our team arrives fully equipped for the job.

How do I pay?

We accept Zelle, cash or check.
